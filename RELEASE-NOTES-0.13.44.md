# Red Faction Guerrilla VR 0.13.44

- Adds five tank camera screens with saved layouts shared across each tank family.
- Tank controls: left stick drives/steers, right stick aims, right trigger fires main gun, left trigger fires secondary. A no longer fires the secondary.
- Artillery gun camera attaches to the physical launcher model, avoiding individual rocket-tube animation.
- Heavy/artillery body and screens remain hull-facing; medium cabin follows traversal.
- Tank front/gun screens update every game frame; side/rear feeds rotate at one third rate. APC front screen updates every game frame, with the other feeds rotating.
- Includes approved camera, grip, reload, HUD and eye placements as fresh-install defaults. Updates preserve saved settings.
- Removes temporary tank camera editors, camera markers and test-spawn shortcuts from the release build.

Known issue: excessive sun/sky glare can still appear while looking upward. The advanced lighting work is not a complete replacement of every engine lighting path. Camera placement and performance vary with scene load; no universal FPS gain is claimed.

Install: extract RFGRVR-Setup.zip and run RFGRVR-Setup.exe, or download the standalone installer. Launch through Steam and select VR. Standard mode remains available.

This mod is AI-authored under human direction. Free, noncommercial and open access under the included community license. Includes third-party license notices; requires your own supported Steam game installation.
